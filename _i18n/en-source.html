What it does

A season on the water, written down by itself

Dockbook works while you’re out on the water. It notices when you leave the dock, keeps watch while you’re at anchor, and turns every outing into a page of your boat’s history.

Automatic trips

Leave the dock. The logbook fills itself in.

Dockbook detects when you leave your marina or anchorage and records the trip in the background, then names it after your saved places or the nearest marina, bay or town.

  • Route, time, distance, speeds and fuel for every outing
  • Weather and water levels recorded along the way, hour by hour
  • A logbook page you can share as a PDF, and a replay on the map

Anchor watch

Sleep at anchor. Dockbook keeps the watch.

Mark the anchor where it went down, or ahead of the bow with the compass, then choose the swing circle. The alarm sounds if the boat starts dragging, even with the screen locked.

  • GPS smoothing to avoid false alarms
  • Wind and storm alerts for where you’re anchored
  • Map, marine chart or satellite under the circle

Weather & wind

Weather forecasts tailored to your boat

Set the wind, gust and wave limits (when available) that suit your boat and crew. Every hour of the next ten days is colour-coded green, yellow, orange or red against those limits.

  • Radar, rain forecast, wind and waves on one map with one time slider
  • Real-time readings from the nearest buoys and stations
  • The best time to leave along a planned route

Fuel, maintenance and true cost

Know what your boat really costs, per hour

Log fill-ups and services in seconds, scan the receipt, and let Dockbook remind you when the next oil change or impeller replacement is due, by engine hours or by date.

  • Service intervals per engine, with serial numbers and parts
  • A to-do list linked to what you need to buy for each job
  • The real cost per hour and per season, insurance and slip included

Safety gear and papers

Ready for a safety check

Dockbook identifies the equipment required for your boat’s size and waters, based on Transport Canada and U.S. Coast Guard rules, and tells you what’s missing or about to expire.

  • Flares, extinguishers, life jackets and lights, counted and dated
  • Licence, registration and insurance, with renewal reminders
  • A safety checklist to print or show on the phone

Questions

Before you cast off

Does Dockbook work without cellular coverage?

Yes. Trips, the anchor watch and your logbook work offline. Weather and charts load when you have a connection, and trips get their weather once you’re back in range.

What boats does Dockbook support?

Powerboats, sailboats and personal watercraft, with one or several engines: outboard, sterndrive, inboard or saildrive. You can keep several boats.

Can I use Dockbook on a Wi‑Fi‑only iPad?

Yes, for everything at the dock. A Wi‑Fi‑only iPad has no GPS, so let your iPhone record the trips and watch the anchor: they appear on the iPad through iCloud.

Can I bring my existing logbook?

Import trips from GPX files and your records from a spreadsheet (CSV). Dockbook matches them to your boat.

Is it a navigation app?

No. Dockbook’s charts and weather are for planning. Always carry official charts and all the safety equipment required for your boat and waters.
